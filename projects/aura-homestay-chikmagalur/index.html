Project Overview & Engineering Highlights

Aura Homestay is located amidst the tranquil coffee estates of Chikmagalur. The project required careful geotechnical stabilization on sloping terrain, custom RCC framework, and open-plan cottage layouts designed to capture natural cross-ventilation and mountain vistas.

Manidhay managed the entire lifecycle: soil investigation, architectural floor planning, 3D exterior renderings, reinforced foundation footing, complete civil brickwork and roofing, and MEP layout integration.
