Project Overview & Modular Alignment

The Hosur project demonstrated the extreme versatility and fast-track turnaround of Manidhay’s modular concrete wall panels. The site demanded high boundary fencing that could resist high wind loads and require near-zero long term plaster maintenance.

Precast aerated panels were slotted onto engineered foundation posts, producing a crisp, clean aesthetic that perfectly demarcates property lines.
