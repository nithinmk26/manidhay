About the Podcast

"Civil Engineering Made Simple" bridges the gap between complex engineering textbooks and the everyday challenges faced by homeowners, developers, and young engineers. From foundational soil mechanics to concrete technology and cost management, each episode breaks down crucial topics in clear, accessible language.

Key Topics & Discussion Areas

Structural Safety & IS Codes

Why standard thumb rules fail and how certified calculations prevent cracks, deflection, and earthquake vulnerabilities.

Precast & Aerated Concrete

How Readymade Aerated Concrete Wall panels speed up construction by 4x while offering superior thermal insulation.

BOQ & Budget Estimation

The mechanics of accurate quantity surveying, PWD SR rates, bank loan valuation, and avoiding contractor cost overruns.

Hillside Construction in Coorg & Chikmagalur

Addressing slope stability, retaining walls, heavy monsoon runoff, and foundation design on rocky and uneven strata.

Soil Testing & Bearing Capacity

Why every building owner must conduct a geotechnical soil investigation before digging foundation trenches.

Careers in Civil Engineering

Practical advice for civil engineering students, diploma holders, and site supervisors on software skills and site mastery.
