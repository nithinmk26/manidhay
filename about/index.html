About Manidhay Business Services

Built on a 3-decade family legacy in construction, delivering unified civil engineering solutions across Karnataka and Pan-India.

Our Journey & Philosophy

One-Point Solutions for All Wings of Civil Engineering

Manidhay Business Services began in the year 2018 in Chikmagalur — the historic town where coffee was first cultivated in India. Our family has been actively rooted in the construction sector for over 3 decades.

We initially pioneered the supply and installation of Aerated Concrete Readymade Wall Panels, helping builders achieve rapid, insulated, and cost-effective wall construction. In 2019, following a strategic knowledge expansion, we consolidated our practice to provide a comprehensive One-Point Service across all branches of Civil Engineering.

With our Head Office at Chikmagalur and a Branch in Bangalore, we serve residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional clients. Driven by a young, disciplined team, we value time as money — utilizing structured online client meetings for rapid approvals and project updates to keep all site timelines on schedule.
