You’re Journey for Last 6 Days

You’re Journey for Last 6 Days

July 8, 2020
by Sri Rama Rao
with no comment
General

The last 6 days have changed most of the things in my life. I have understood the importance of Any Language thought in our school days. Then it was only for school sake to pass the examination but now most of the so-called Digital Marketing Business depends on Language, Ex: English – A universal language. In general, putting something to communicate in the form of Content plays a vital role in the growth of any kind of business.

I have started viewing and hearing contents of Gary Vaynerchuk, who motivates people to think about themselves and put as much content as we know on Social Media platforms like Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, etc. Also, I got to know that putting content on the Social Media platform earns money which was a surprising fact for me.

Content Writing

Content writing is the most attractive business having huge demand in the market presently. To convey product information, write a blog, sell a product, to give more information about a service, everywhere Content Writers are needed who produce good content that connects people with the product directly.

So, I decided to join a short term course to learn what is content writing and the benefits of it from the Content Vidhya team. After going through this course I got to know that to be a content writer we should have a good hold of the subject in any language.

A piece of content may be in the form of audio, photo, video, or in the form of writing which has to give some information about something. So, I have decided to start a Podcast to put my professional experience as a content in the audio form to reach people.

Podcast

I have never thought that I will start a Podcast. In the last 6 days, it was an eye-opener in most of the digital fields wherein I have learned there are many easy ways to reach people to convey our thoughts and ideas very easily. I have approached people over Linkedin wherein a very good response I have got and invited people from different industrial backgrounds as guests for my podcast episodes.

Instagram

A small photo Content put on Instagram can make our business sales go higher than our expectations. In the last 6 days, I have experimented by putting my services in the form of photo content on instagram to get back to me many enquiries which was not at all expected.

So, finally, I can conclude that in the present digital generation no person needs to travel and advertise his product or services. It can be put in the form of a good Content and can be advertised digitally to reach as many people as possible globally to grow the business. So, make use of good courses in Content Writing and implement it in the Digital Marketing Strategy.
