ABOUT MANIDHAY<\/h2>

One point Solutions for all Civil Engineering Services<\/span><\/h3>
<\/div>

Manidhay Business Services<\/strong> begin in the year 2018 in a small town, Chickmagaluru where coffee was first cultivated in India. We started with our service in the Aerated Concrete Readymade Wall<\/em> panel installation. Our head office is at Chickmagaluru and a branch at Bangalore providing business services globally.<\/p>

In 2019 after a major knowledge explosion, it was decided to provide a full time One point service in all wings of Civil Engineering.<\/p>

We are constantly upgrading our services in a shorter time to grow globally. With a young energetic team, we manifest service on time to keep up the schedule of the project. Respecting the value of time as money<\/em>, we make most of our meetings online with clients for updates and solve issues.<\/p>

To make Civil and Structural Engineering simple we have come up with a Blog, YouTube Channel, Instagram, Facebook Page, and Podcast<\/em>. To reach people and make them understand the necessity of hiring an engineer for their project.<\/p><\/div><\/div>\n <\/section>\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t<\/section>\n\t\t\t\t
